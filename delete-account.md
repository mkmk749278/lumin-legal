 
# Delete your Lumin account

**Last updated:** 2026-05-20

Lumin gives you two ways to delete your account and the data we hold for you.

## Method 1 — In-app (recommended; instant)

1. Open the Lumin app
2. Tap the **Menu** icon (top-right or bottom-right) → **Settings**
3. Scroll to the **ACCOUNT** section
4. Tap **Delete account**
5. Read the confirmation dialog
6. Type `DELETE` to confirm
7. Tap **Delete account**

Your account and all associated data are removed within seconds. You will be signed out and returned to the welcome screen.

## Method 2 — Email request

If you can't open the app, email **mulakapati446@gmail.com** with subject "Delete my Lumin account" and your phone number. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your phone number and any display-name / country / currency preferences
- Your Telegram chat ID (if linked)
- Your Binance API key (revoked and the encrypted blob removed)
- All per-user preferences (position notional, leverage cap, max concurrent positions, symbol allowlist, pre-TP settings, invalidation settings)
- Your trade activity history older than 12 months is purged automatically; the rest is removed at account deletion

## What is NOT deleted

- **Funds in your Binance account** — Lumin never holds your funds and cannot withdraw them. They remain on Binance after our account is deleted. We strongly recommend you also revoke our API key on Binance directly after deleting your Lumin account.
- **Anonymous, aggregated signal-quality metrics** that have already been de-identified before the deletion request (we cannot re-link these to you)
- **Crash logs from Firebase Crashlytics** retained for up to 90 days by Google
